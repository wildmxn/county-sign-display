What We Do

Signage, manufactured and installed locally.

Every job below is designed, produced and installed by our own team at 6 Booth St, Minden, no middleman, no shipping wait.

Commercial Signs

Storefront signs, building signage and site identification for businesses across the Haliburton Highlands.

From hanging signs and channel letters to freestanding site signs, we design, fabricate and install on-site, built to hold up to Highlands winters.

Residential Signs

Address signs, property markers and custom home signage for cottages and homes throughout Minden Hills.

One-off custom work, made to match your property, not a template off a catalogue.

Vehicle Graphics

Fleet lettering, decals and full graphics packages that turn work trucks, vans and trailers into moving billboards.

We've wrapped trucks and trailers for local trades, contractors and security companies across the region.

Lettering & Decals

Custom cut lettering and decals for windows, doors and displays, commercial or residential, one-off or in bulk.

Great for storefront hours, promotions, safety decals and small-batch branding jobs.
